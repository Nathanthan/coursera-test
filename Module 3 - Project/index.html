Our Menu

Chicken

Our Chicken menu features delicious and flavorful dishes prepared with fresh ingredients.

Try our classic grilled chicken served with vegetables and rice. It is a simple but satisfying meal that is perfect for lunch or dinner.

We also offer crispy fried chicken with our special house seasoning. The chicken is crispy on the outside and tender on the inside.

For something lighter, our chicken salad combines fresh lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, and grilled chicken with a delicious dressing.

Every chicken dish is prepared with care to provide a delicious meal for our customers.

Beef

Our Beef menu offers hearty meals made for customers who enjoy rich and flavorful dishes.

Our classic beef steak is grilled to perfection and served with vegetables and a side of rice.

You can also try our beef burger, made with a juicy beef patty, fresh lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, and our special sauce.

Another favorite is our beef stir-fry, combining tender beef strips with fresh vegetables and a savory sauce.

Our beef dishes are perfect for anyone looking for a filling and satisfying meal.

Sushi

Our Sushi menu offers a selection of fresh and colorful Japanese-inspired dishes.

Try our California Roll made with crab, cucumber, and avocado wrapped in seasoned rice and seaweed.
